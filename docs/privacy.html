Privacy policy

Dust — CW Morse trainer
Android package dev.dust.morse
Effective date: 17 October 2026

This policy covers the Dust app on Android, and the same program on the web at mashu.github.io/dust and on desktop. It describes what the app stores, what leaves the device, and who receives it.

The short version

Dust stores your practice on the device you are using. It has no account and no server of its own. The only data that leaves the device is the request the app makes to Google to load its typefaces.

Who publishes Dust

Dust is an open-source Morse trainer. The source and this policy are published at github.com/mashu/dust. There is no account system, and the developer does not receive your practice history.

What the app stores on your device

Dust stores the following on the device so practice can continue the next time you open it:

  • Settings. Speed, character set, session length, keyer, tone, band conditions, theme, and whether the guided setup has been opened, so it opens by itself only the first time.
  • Practice history. For each session: the date and time, the groups that were sent, the characters you typed, accuracy, scores, speed, and per-letter results. The app keeps the newest 500 sessions.
  • Level counters. Counts used to decide when to raise or lower the level you are practising.

On Android this stays in the app’s private storage for dev.dust.morse. Other apps cannot read it. Dust does not use it for advertising, and it does not send it to the developer.

The web app keeps the same information in that site’s local storage. Desktop builds keep it in the app’s data folder. Nothing is synced between devices or between installs.

How that information is used

It is used only to run the trainer: to play sessions at the settings you chose, to score what you typed, to show your history and statistics, and to adjust the level when you have turned that on. It is not used to identify you, and it is not combined with anyone else’s data.

What leaves the device

Dust does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting software. It does not ask you to sign in.

The app does request its typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). That request includes your IP address and the ordinary technical data a connection carries, such as the browser or web-view identifier. Your settings and practice history are not included. Google receives that request and handles it under Google’s privacy policy. Google is the only third party the app sends data to.

If you install Dust from Google Play, Google also handles the download and the store listing under its own policies. That collection is Google’s, not a feature of the app.

Microphone, location, and other sensitive data

Dust plays sound through the speaker or headphones. It does not record the microphone. Paddle practice uses the keyboard or on-screen buttons.

Dust does not access your location, contacts, photos, messages, or files outside its own storage. It does not collect payment information. There are no purchases in the app.

Children

Dust is a general-audience practice tool. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

The history stays on the device until you remove it. The app keeps at most the newest 500 sessions; older ones are discarded as you practise.

  • Android. Uninstall Dust, or clear the app’s storage in system settings. That deletes the settings and the history.
  • Web. Clear site data for https://mashu.github.io.
  • Desktop. Delete the Dust data folder for your system.

There is no account to delete, because the app never receives a copy.

Sharing and sale

Dust does not sell personal information. It does not share practice history with anyone. The only party that receives data from the app is Google, and only the font request described above. Dust does not share data with advertising networks or data brokers.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will replace this page and the effective date at the top will change. The app links here from Settings.

Contact
